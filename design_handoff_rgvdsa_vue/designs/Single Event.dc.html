About this event

The general meeting is where the whole chapter comes together. We hear back from every committee, debate and vote on new business, and set priorities for the weeks ahead. It's the single best place to understand what RGV-DSA is working on right now — and to weigh in.

Not a member yet? You're still welcome. Visitors can observe, ask questions, and meet organizers. Members in good standing may vote. We'll do a quick round of introductions at the top so no one feels lost.

Agenda

  1. 1Welcome & introductions — new folks say hello.
  2. 2Committee report-backs — Mutual Aid, Labor, Political Ed, Electoral.
  3. 3New business & votes — proposals on the floor for a member vote.
  4. 4Fall planning & announcements — what's next, and how to plug in.

Good to know

  • Doors open at 1:30 PM — come early to grab coffee and say hi.
  • Bring a notebook if you like; the agenda and minutes are shared afterward.
  • Snacks provided. Bring your own water bottle if you can.
  • Running late or joining online? No problem — slip in whenever.
Accessibility & childcare

The venue is wheelchair accessible with an accessible restroom. Free childcare is available on request, and Spanish interpretation will be provided. Need another accommodation? Email events@rgvdsa.org at least 48 hours ahead and we'll make it happen.
